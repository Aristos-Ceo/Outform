PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open the OUTFORM-site folder in VS Code.
3. Use the Live Server extension to open index.html. A local server is recommended because the project uses local assets and browser storage.

WHAT WAS UPDATED
- Refined the signature black, warm ivory, and burnt-orange palette.
- Reworked responsive sizing and spacing for the hero, product grid, cards, story blocks, and page heroes.
- Homepage now shows ten featured sneaker products.
- Product cards use one circular plus control for quick add and a clean product link; quick add asks the customer to select a size.

IMPORTANT DEMO AND LAUNCH NOTES
- This remains a front-end portfolio/demo store. It does not collect or verify payments, provide real bank account details, or authenticate accounts.
- Bank transfer and card options currently express the customer's preferred payment method and ask the team for verified instructions; they are not connected to a payment provider.
- Never mark an order paid until a trusted provider webhook or verified backend confirms payment.
- Product names, pricing, sizing, stock and brand associations are illustrative demo data and must be checked before commercial use.
- The existing local SVG logo files are illustrative artwork, not verified official brand-logo downloads. Replace them with approved official assets before commercial launch.
- Some editorial images are local campaign/product stills rather than photographs of models wearing the shoes. No unverified customer reviews have been added; the homepage editorial statement is explicitly labelled as a brand point of view.
- Newsletter and contact forms use a mailto handoff rather than a connected CRM or mailing-list backend.
- Confirm WhatsApp contact details and business configuration before launch.
